The model allocated to one or more stocks in **95.42%** of weekly periods. Its total one-sided turnover measure was **309.93** over the holdout (sum of absolute portfolio allocation changes, including initial and final liquidation).

## Reproducibility concern

On a newer GitHub Actions execution, the **unchanged V2 pipeline** reported **+29.74%** in its historical holdout, whereas an earlier execution reported **-19.15%** over the same date range. A source revision or numerical/model instability is possible, but the cause was not established. To address this, the V3 branch now records SHA-256 hashes of each generated CSV and verifies repeatability of V3 using an identical downloaded dataset. Do not treat the V3 result as durable until reproducibility and out-of-sample data quality have been confirmed.

## Key scientific limitations

- V2 already examined approximately this same 2021–2026 historical period. Therefore V3's final slice is **not truly new, prospective evidence** for the overall project; design decisions may already reflect what we learned about that period.
- The ticker universe is composed of present-day survivors; delisted stocks and historically changing index membership are absent (**survivorship bias**).
- The strategy's strong returns may reflect concentration in high-growth stocks rather than repeatable short-horizon forecasting skill; ticker attribution should be added.
- Split- and dividend-adjusted opening prices are not executable market quotes. Slippage, variable spreads, taxes, liquidity constraints and broker order handling are not modeled.
- The ensemble disagreement gate is an uncertainty *heuristic*, not a calibrated confidence interval.
- **Do not execute real-money buys/sells** based on this historical result. Next meaningful test is logged paper trading on fresh future dates, with code/model parameters frozen.
